01 · Overview

What Culture Capsules are — and why now.

Capsules are premium thematic micro-courses (Coco Chanel · Louis XIV · Impressionism · etc.) priced as standalone products and bundled into FA0 / FA1 as upsells. Not language lessons — culture, story, art, food, history — taught in approachable French with English support, anchored in cinematic content already produced.

◆

The product

A 6-week themed capsule. Live cohort + on-demand lessons + a beautifully designed digital workbook. Each capsule = one cultural world.

◆

The buyer

FA0 / FA1 students wanting depth between levels, francophiles who don't want grammar, and gift-buyers (a Capsule is a "Hermès scarf" of education).

◆

The wedge

No competitor sells luxury, themed, finite French cultural courses. Most are language drills. We sell taste.
