Our Mission

Why The French Atelier exists.

Most adult French programs teach textbook grammar, not living language. The French Atelier was built by ACADOMIA, France's leading private learning network, to give serious adult learners what no one else offers: live native teachers, real French culture, and a method that produces actual fluency — not classroom French.

Our Promise

Four pledges we keep — every class

100% Certified

Every teacher holds a French university degree in language pedagogy.

100% Live

No pre-recorded videos. Every class is a real, two-way conversation.

100% Cultural

Each lesson begins in a real place: a café, museum, market, or street.

100% French Native

All teachers are born in France, raised in France, living in France.

The Program

A certified live course,
unlike anything else

The French Atelier runs in small live Zoom groups of 10 to 12 learners per class. A certified native teacher leads, supported by a custom-built curriculum, a structured 6-level path through the Common European Framework (A1 to C2), and the freedom that comes from learning by speaking — not from filling worksheets.

Complete our series of lessons and earn a CEFR-aligned A1 or A2 certificate, issued by Acadomia — France's most trusted name in personalized education. All classes are recorded and available on-demand inside your learning platform, forever.

How It Works

The Six Cultural Pillars

Six worlds through which we teach the French language.

The French Atelier curriculum is built around six great pillars of French cultural life. Together, they constitute an education not just in language — but in a way of being in the world.

Art & Architecture

From the Louvre's vaulted halls to the ateliers of Montmartre.

  • The vocabulary of French museums
  • Impressionism and its cultural language
  • Haussmann's Paris and urban beauty
  • Cathedral French — Chartres, Reims, Notre-Dame
  • Contemporary French design and savoir-faire

Gastronomy & Wine

The language of the market, the cellar, the table.

  • The rituals of the French meal
  • Regional produce and their expressions
  • Wine vocabulary from terroir to tasting
  • The boulangerie, the fromagerie, the marché
  • Restaurant French in practice

Travel & Landmarks

Speak your way across France, region by region.

  • Navigating French cities with confidence
  • Regional accents and local dialects
  • The vocabulary of French transport
  • Iconic landmarks and their cultural context
  • Travel French: hotels, stations, countryside

Fashion & Film

The codes of elegance, on screen and on the street.

  • The language of the French fashion house
  • Nouvelle Vague and cinematic French
  • Icons of French style and their lexicon
  • The Cannes Film Festival in French
  • Everyday Parisian elegance and its idiom

Music & Poetry

From the chanson to the verse that shaped a nation.

  • Édith Piaf, Brel, Barbara — the chanson tradition
  • French poetry and its natural rhythms
  • Contemporary French music and rap
  • Lyrics as language instruction
  • Opera, classical music, and French expression

Tradition & History

The rituals and stories behind every French word.

  • Bastille Day and the language of the Republic
  • French holidays, ceremonies, and their vocabulary
  • The French Revolution and its living legacy
  • Family life and generational transmission
  • The sacred and the secular in French culture

Culture in Motion

French life, as it's lived

Not as a museum. Not as a stereotype. As it happens — every day — in homes, kitchens, salons, and streets across France.
