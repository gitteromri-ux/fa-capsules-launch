Who We Are

Thirty-five years of educational excellence

Acadomia was founded in 1989 with a singular conviction: that excellence in education comes from expert instruction, delivered with rigour and care. In the decades since, it has become one of France's leading private education groups — a name synonymous with academic rigour, intellectual ambition, and high standards of teaching, from Strasbourg to Nice, from Paris to the world.

Headquartered in the 8th arrondissement of Paris — the heart of the city's educational and professional life — Acadomia operates at the intersection of academic rigour and genuine human connection. It is this institutional heritage, built over more than three decades, that gives The French Atelier its authority and its standing among adult French learners worldwide.

From the Loire Valley to the boulevards of Paris, Acadomia's teachers are rooted in the life of France.

The Acadomia Philosophy

Expert instruction, the Acadomia standard

Acadomia's founding insight was simple but radical: the finest learning happens in small, expertly-led groups, with an instructor who truly understands your level, your pace, and your goals. This philosophy of personalized, expert instruction is the institute's signature — and it is the same philosophy that shapes The French Atelier's intimate live classes of just 10–12 adult learners.

When you study with The French Atelier, you inherit more than three decades of pedagogical authority. The standards, the academic credibility, the insistence on genuine progress — all of it flows from Acadomia's long-standing reputation as one of France's leading private education groups.

The Connection

Why Acadomia built
The French Atelier

For more than thirty years, Acadomia has stood for excellence and prestige in private education across France. The French Atelier is the natural extension of that authority to the world — lending its name and its academic credibility to a culturally-rooted French programme designed for adult lifelong learners everywhere who wish to truly speak French. Not grammar-French. Not textbook-French. Living, breathing, contextual French.

When you enroll in The French Atelier, you do not simply sign up for a language course. You join a tradition — a community of learners and teachers united by a love of France and the conviction that language is inseparable from culture.
