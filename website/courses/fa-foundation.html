What you'll learn

By unit 20, you will…

Foundation takes you from silence to your first real exchanges in French — the milestones below are what A1.1 looks like in practice.

  • Greet, introduce yourself and say where you’re from with correct pronunciation
  • Order food and drink, and pay, in a Paris café or bakery
  • Count, tell the time, and handle prices, dates and phone numbers
  • Ask simple questions and understand short, slow replies
  • Master the French sounds that trip up beginners (nasals, the French R, liaison)
  • Describe your family, your home and your daily routine in the present tense

The full curriculum

Twenty units, one unforgettable first weekend

A structured A0→A1.1 curriculum, each 85-minute live unit themed to a real moment of Parisian life.

01

Se présenter

Greet, exchange names, and propose the use of tu with s’appelle. Discover the cultural codes of first meetings in French.

02

Épeler & vérifier une information

Ask how to spell a word, spell it back, and confirm or correct using clarification strategies. Practice the French alphabet and phonetics.

03

Demander son chemin

Use excusez-moi and c’est où ? to ask for directions. Understand simple responses (left, right, straight on) and key vocabulary for public places.

04

Réparer + commander une boisson simple

Signal a communication breakdown, ask for repetition or slower speech, and place a simple order at a café.

05

Commander [1/5] — Je voudrais… + c’est combien ?

Order food and drinks using je voudrais, ask for the price, and handle numbers 0–39. Discover French restaurant culture.

06

Commander [2/5] — Autre chose ?

Respond to Autre chose ? and C’est tout ?, add items to an order, and practice numbers 40–69. Explore French café and brasserie menus.

07

Proposer une sortie simple

Use être and the pronoun y to suggest an outing, accept or decline, and make a simple decision.

08

Accepter / refuser + exprimer une préférence

Use je préfère to choose between two options, accept or refuse politely, and reach a clear decision. Learn expressions of agreement and disagreement.

09

Échanger un numéro de téléphone

Ask for and give a phone number, verify it using c’est…, c’est ça ?, and practice numbers 70–99. Understand French mobile number conventions.

10

Commander [3/5] — Payer par carte

Express hunger and thirst with j’ai faim / soif, place an order, and pay by card. Practice a full café interaction from start to finish.

11

Acheter un billet / demander un prix

Buy a ticket, ask for the price, and pay. Consolidate je voudrais and payment vocabulary. Discover the cultural role of museums in France.

12

Donner / échanger email & adresse

Exchange email addresses and street addresses using possessive adjectives (mon, ma). Practice spelling key details and confirming information.

13

Parler de son origine / où on habite

Use venir de and habiter à to talk about where you are from and where you live. Learn to introduce a third person.

14

Gérer un imprévu

Handle an unexpected situation (a closed venue), propose an alternative plan, and make a decision. Practice nous sommes / ils sont and reservation vocabulary.

15

Fixer un rendez-vous (date / heure)

Propose a time and date, negotiate with ça va ?, confirm, and close the exchange. Learn to tell the time and use days of the week in context.

16

Commander [4/5] — Réactivation

Revisit ordering sequences at a faster pace: respond to Autre chose ? in three different ways, order one or two items, and close the interaction politely.

17

Commander [5/5] — Je prends… + l’addition

Use je prends, react to an unavailable item, and ask for the bill. Complete a fluent end-to-end restaurant interaction.

18

Exprimer une préférence + justifier

Choose between two options, say je préfère…, and justify with parce que c’est + adjectif. Practice opinion exchanges and adjective agreement.

19

Arrivée — Se présenter + orientation + réparation [1/2]

Chain two micro-tasks — introducing yourself and asking for directions — with a repair strategy if needed. Best-of review of units 1–3.

20

Commander + payer + réparer + conclure [2/2]

Complete a full ordering and payment scenario at speed, using at least one correct formula and closing politely. Best-of review of units 4–18.
