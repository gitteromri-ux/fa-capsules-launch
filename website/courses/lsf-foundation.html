The DNA of this course

A conversation, not a classroom

Let’s Speak French is built around one belief: you learn to speak by speaking. Every lesson is real French, lived out loud.

01

Speak first

No grammar drills. You learn by listening and speaking in real situations — French goes in through your ears and out through your mouth.

02

Pair work, small-group exchanges and role-plays in every single lesson. You’re talking, not taking notes.

03

Repeat, recycle and extend key phrases until they’re instinctive. You leave each lesson sounding more like yourself, in French.

The full programme

Ten lessons, ten real moments of French life

Every 85-minute lesson feels like a real moment of French life — no grammar to memorise, only French to live and speak.

01

Hello, je m’appelle…

Hello, I'm called…

Introduce yourself to a stranger: give your first name, country of origin, and language(s) spoken. Use je m’appelle… / je suis… / je parle… and practice the spoken alphabet.

02

Bonjour, bonsoir, au revoir !

Hello, Good Evening, Goodbye!

Greet appropriately at different times of day and take leave. Learn key salutation formulas for morning, afternoon, and evening, and get a first feel for the tu / vous distinction.

03

0 à 20 — les chiffres

0 to 20 — the numbers

Say your age, give a phone number, and understand a simple price. Count from 0 to 20 using j’ai X ans, and practice the nasal sounds that shape French pronunciation.

04

C’est quoi ?

What is it?

Name everyday objects and say what something is. Use c’est un… / une… / des… with 20 common nouns and develop an implicit feel for grammatical gender.

05

Les couleurs et les descriptions

Colours and descriptions

Describe an object or a person using one or two adjectives. Learn colour vocabulary alongside grand / petit / beau / vieux and develop an implicit awareness of adjective agreement.

06

⚡ Speed dating — Let’s recap !

Speed dating recap

Introduce yourself to three different people in two minutes each. A mid-course review of Units 1–5 with no new language: consolidate your name, country, language, age, and a description in a lively, timed format.

Recap
07

Au café

At the café

Order a drink and pay at a café. Use je voudrais… / je prends… / s’il vous plaît / merci / c’est combien ? in a short, polite exchange.

08

Les jours et les moments

Days and moments

Say which day and time of day you do an activity. Learn the 7 days of the week and time-of-day expressions (le matin, le midi, l’après-midi, le soir, la nuit).

09

J’aime / je n’aime pas

I like / I don't like

Express your feelings about 5 activities or foods. Use the full opinion scale — j’adore / j’aime / je n’aime pas / je déteste — with an infinitive or a noun.

10

À bientôt !

See you soon!

Hold a short closing conversation: introduce yourself, share a preference, and mention a simple plan. A general review of all LSF Foundation language with complete politeness formulas. The final assessed task of the course.
