Five Steps

Your complete Atelier journey

From a first conversation to a certificate that carries the weight of a 35-year institution — five steps, one continuous experience.

01

Assessment & Enrollment

Choose your track after a live placement call with a French Atelier advisor. This is not a written test — it is a conversation.

Our advisors listen for where you are in your French life and place you in the course where you will thrive, not merely survive. Step Two · Your Digital Home
02

Join the Learning Platform

Your dashboard, schedule, course materials and community — all in one elegant, purpose-built environment. No downloads, no complexity.

The platform is the Atelier beyond the live class: where recordings live, where study materials await, where the community gathers between sessions, and where Julien, your 24/7 AI French coach, is always on call. Just a laptop and a microphone, and France opens to you.

Personal dashboard Schedule & calendar Cultural resources Julien AI access
Step Three · The Heart of It
03

Attend Weekly Live Classes

85-minute live lessons with native certified teachers, broadcast from iconic locations in France. Small groups of 10–12 make every class a genuine conversation — never a lecture.

Each lesson begins in a real cultural place — a market, a gallery, a village square — and uses that context to teach the French of lived experience. Times are calibrated across timezones, so wherever you are in the world, France is always within reach.

85 minutes 10–12 learners Native certified teachers Live from France
Step Four · Deepening the Learning
04

Practice & Reinforcement

Every lesson is recorded and available on demand. Between live classes, complete structured micro-tasks and join a weekly study group to keep your momentum alive.

Julien bridges the gaps — available at any hour for pronunciation drills, vocabulary practice, cultural questions and spontaneous conversation. The French Atelier experience does not end when the live class closes; it continues until the next one begins.

All classes recorded On-demand replay Weekly study groups Julien AI · 24/7

This is not a participation ribbon. It is a verified mark of communicative attainment, issued by Acadomia — one of France’s leading private education groups — carrying the weight of a 35-year institution. It marks a new relationship with the French language, and the world that French unlocks.

The CEFR Ladder

Climb from A0 to A2.2, course by course

Each structured course completes one defined arc of the Common European Framework — and each one travels a different region of France. Progress is visible, measurable, and certified by Acadomia.

A0 → A1.1 FA Foundation Your first French — sounds, greetings, the present tense. Paris
A1.1 → A1.2 FA Beginner Everyday exchanges — describing, asking, narrating simply. Normandy → Paris
A1.2 → A2.1 FA Elementary Past and future — plans, memories, opinions in context. Loire → Atlantic
A2.1 → A2.2 FA Intermediate Confident conversation — nuance, register, real social life. Marseille · Alsace
Certified Acadomia Certificate A CEFR-aligned certificate at every 20-lesson milestone. Verified attainment
